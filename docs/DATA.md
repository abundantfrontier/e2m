## Rebuilding

1. Starter sweep: `python -m e2m.data.sweep --tasks tasks/starter.jsonl --out data --jobs 16`.
2. Download ExeBench `train_real_simple_io` (https://huggingface.co/datasets/jordiae/exebench) to `datasets/exebench/` and an AnghaBench sample (https://github.com/brenocfg/AnghaBench) to `datasets/angha/`.
3. Scaled pool: `python -m e2m.scale --target 3000 --jobs 16` (needs a local Ollama with `qwen3:14b`), then `python -m e2m.data.sweep --tasks tasks/scaled.jsonl --out data/scaled --compilers gcc,clang --grid reduced --jobs 16 --llvm-seeds 0`.
4. Spec rewrites and the training mix: `python -m e2m.loop rewrite` and `python -m e2m.loop mix`; round pipelines build their own derived files (see the README).
5. Per-task model outputs (`eval.jsonl`, `loop.jsonl`, `hybrid.jsonl`) are regenerated by the eval and loop commands.
